# Power BI Competitor Sales Analysis

## Overview

This project focused on analyzing and comparing a manufacturer's sales performance against competitors across multiple geographies.

Using Power BI, Power Query, and DAX, the project follows a complete business intelligence workflow from data integration and transformation to analysis, visualization, and storytelling. The objective is to deliver actionable insights that help business users understand product performance, market share, sales trends, and competitive positioning.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze manufacturer sales performance
- Compare sales results against competitors
- Measure market share across different regions
- Identify product growth opportunities
- Track sales trends over time
- Build interactive reports for business stakeholders
- Create a data-driven story to support decision-making
